A localized error-handling regression added one family and six leaves before checking the governed proof estate. All six cases ran, but the full gate then correctly refused the undeclared family, missing admission and zero-growth budget violation. Focused product tests alone had not checked the repository's independent inventory contract.

The cases were incorporated as explicit subcases of the existing transaction-atomicity witness, preserving every original assertion, and its declared contract and oracle were strengthened. The frozen counts and budget stayed unchanged; all 33 core and governance tests passed. Check proof inventory and admission during focused iteration before paying for a full gate whenever adding or changing executable tests. Existing policy already mandates admission; the proposed improvement is an earlier workflow witness, not a new budget exception. This is an unratified candidate.
